# HomeStudio R116 — complete GLB furniture and forced graphics engines

The shared Meshopt-compressed GLB contains complete double/single beds, a sofa,
armchair, coffee/dining/desk tables, dining/office/bar chairs, bedside table,
potted plant, basin faucet, catalogue-shaped kitchen tap, media unit and TV.
The sofa uses adapted, scanned creased upholstery. Beds have shaped layered
cloth and piping. CC0 source licenses, author credits and transformations are in
public-view/assets/furniture-r116/PROVENANCE.json and accompanying licenses.
The model library also retains two small reusable cloth component meshes.

Public tours, personal tours, the editable apartment and kitchen await the same
library. Repeated parts share geometry and PBR materials. District furniture
uses instances across apartments/buildings instead of duplicating mesh vertices.
Mirrored buildings bake corrected winding and keep positive instance matrices.
Invisible lightweight envelopes own walking collision; detailed folds and leaves
are visual surfaces. Existing positions, master-bed direction, maps and custom
sizes remain controlled by the existing plan. Architectural cabinetry and its
operable drawers/doors, measured sanitary products and structural walls retain
their engineering geometry. The models are illustrative, not exact purchased
furniture or scans of this house.

Options > graphics engine offers Auto, Forced WebGPU/TSL and Forced WebGL2.
The preference persists in this browser. Auto may recover with WebGL2; forced
WebGPU reports unsupported/device failure without silently switching engines.
A visible recovery action offers Auto or engine selection. Engine changes retain
the tour/orbit viewpoint. WebXR uses Auto/WebGL2; forced WebGPU explains this.
