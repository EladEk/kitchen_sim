# Home Studio R136

Broad matte grey surrounds are reconstructed around the repeating main sun-balcony bays: vertical returns, central dividers where the two north bays meet, horizontal beams and the exposed balcony-edge fascias. The surrounding pale limestone, interior walls and upper roof-terrace guards retain their previous finishes.

Placement follows the original floor polygons; the grey return stops before the actual enclosed wall. Profile widths, heights and colour are photographic estimates. No specific grey product, exact RAL or measured elevation is asserted. The district still uses its existing 4102-based geometry for neighbouring copies, including the separate C5 buildings: these are shared finish reconstructions, not newly surveyed building-specific elevations.

The grey material is shared, matte and independent of the stone-course shader. It survives the public allowlist, WebGL restoration, WebGPU conversion and photo conversion. Main-front fascias are replaced by the new beam rather than doubled; touching beam sections are unioned. Vertical return collision and floor-map outlines follow the actual new geometry. Existing source wall buffers and openings are retained.

References: the three new owner photographs, earlier exterior/garden images and official Efi Capital stage-A gallery.
