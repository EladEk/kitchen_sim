# Home Studio R133

The elevator HUD and model call panels share one action flow. A nearby press opens the floor selector immediately and pauses walking, without scene preparation. A remote press approaches the selected car and automatically opens the selector after preparation succeeds. Floor selection keeps the actual building and car. Invalid stops and interrupted approaches do not open stale dialogs. Modal input pauses automatic walking-area preparation.

District and walking displays consolidate overlapping rectangular stone faces, including the thin veneer and the matching structural wall face beneath it. A render-only union uses the outer plane within a 16 mm layer tolerance, preserves apertures and leaves original collision and selection geometry intact. Exposed stone returns are classified as exterior in the merged display, including those on source interior-wall batches. One shared stone material and its textures serve the district.

Shared lobby and lift waiting-area walls use warm brown-beige (#c5ad91); lobby panelling uses #ad9074. Apartment wall finishes, ceiling finishes and entrance-door colours are preserved.
